"Lawyers must assess the terms and conditions of using a generative AI platform or service to understand the security of the information being inputted, how that information is being used by the platform or service, and what data sources the platform or service is using."
— Missouri Informal Opinion 2024-11 (April 2024)

What this means in practice

Every AI tool handles your data differently. Some train on it. Some delete it immediately. Using the wrong tool for the wrong task is an ethics violation. Same product, different plan = completely different legal framework. Free Copilot trains on your data and targets ads. M365 Copilot keeps everything in your tenant.

The 4 Tiers of Data Protection

Tier 1 — Consumer Default

Trains on your data. ChatGPT Free, Claude Free, Gemini personal. Your prompts become the product.

Tier 2 — Training Off

Consumer tool with training toggled off. Better, but still governed by ToS the vendor can change anytime.

Tier 3 — API / DPA

Bilateral contract. DPA. No training. The vendor is a data processor, not a controller. Anthropic API, Reduct.

Tier 4 — Enterprise Tenancy

Data stays in your managed tenant. Enterprise agreement + DPA. Admin controls. M365 Copilot, Google Workspace Gemini.

Two questions determine the risk

How sensitive is the data? + How protected is the tool?

Safe combinations

Low data + Any tier
General legal research, public case law, statutory analysis. No client info. Use whatever tool you want.

Dangerous combinations

High data + Tier 1 or 2
Police reports, jail calls, client confessions, discovery in a consumer AI tool. Ethics violation.

Consumer AI chats are not privileged

United States v. Heppner (S.D.N.Y. Feb. 2026)

A client used consumer Claude AI on his own to research his defense, then shared the results with his lawyers. The court ordered production — "simply no reasonable expectation of confidentiality" under consumer ToS.

What this means for attorneys

This was a client's chats, not an attorney's — but the reasoning reaches you too. If consumer ToS destroy confidentiality for a client, they destroy it for anyone. Enterprise/API tiers were explicitly distinguished. Full case breakdown

The feedback trap

When you click thumbs up or thumbs down on an AI response, you give the vendor that entire conversation for training — even if you've opted out of general training.

The problem

OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google all retain feedback-associated conversations for up to 5 years. This is not opt-outable.

The rule

Never provide feedback on any conversation that contains client information. The thumbs up/down button is a consent button.

Toggle training off — do it now

Open your phone. Follow along.

  • ChatGPT: Settings → Data Controls → "Improve the model for everyone" → OFF
  • Claude: Settings → Privacy → "Help improve Claude" → OFF
  • Gemini: myactivity.google.com → Gemini Apps → "Gemini Apps Activity" → OFF
  • Copilot (free): Settings → Privacy → "Optional Connected Experiences" → OFF

This takes 30 seconds and moves you from Tier 1 to Tier 2

It's not enough for client data (that needs Tier 3+), but it stops your general research from becoming training data. Do this on every AI tool you use.

  • 8:00 AM — Email triage. M365 Copilot summarizes overnight emails. (Tier 4)

Demo: Two-stage drafting

Keeps client data out of Tier 1 tools

  • Stage 1 (Any Tier): Research the law. No client facts.
  • Stage 2 (Tier 3+ only): Draft with client facts using a DPA-protected tool.

Why this works

Stage 1 uses the best free tools for what they're good at (research). Stage 2 uses protected tools only when client data enters the picture. You get the best of both without the ethics risk.

Discovery analysis (Tier 3+)

Polish a draft (Any Tier)

"Review this draft for: (1) passive voice, (2) unnecessary qualifiers, (3) sentences over 30 words, (4) paragraphs over 5 sentences. Suggest specific edits."

Transcription: Reduct vs. Word

  • Reduct (Tier 3) — Accepts video natively. Searchable transcripts linked to timestamps. Click a word, jump to that moment. MSPD has enterprise agreement. Recommended.
  • Word Transcribe (Tier 4) — No video support. Convert to audio first. One file at a time. 300 min/month. Lackluster editing pane. Workable but painful for batch processing.
  • Otter.ai (Tier 1) — Do not use for client audio. Consumer tool. Trains on data.
