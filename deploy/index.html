Missouri Informal Opinion 2024-11 requires lawyers to assess the terms of service, security of inputs, and data practices of any AI tool before using it on client work. Most state bars that have weighed in say something similar. This site provides the resources to do that: a risk framework for sorting tools by data protection level, the terms of service for every major AI vendor, and a summary of where every state falls on the key ethical questions.
