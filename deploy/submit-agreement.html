Why this matters

Every SaaS vendor buries data handling terms in their Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, and Data Processing Agreements. Most lawyers don't read them. The ones who do often find alarming clauses — training on your data, sharing with "partners," retaining content indefinitely, using conversations for advertising.

This page lets you submit vendor agreements anonymously. We'll parse them for the clauses that matter to attorneys and add the findings to the Vendor Details page. No personal information is collected or stored.

What we look for in agreements

  • Training clauses: Does the vendor use your content to train AI models? Can you opt out? Is the opt-out real or performative?
  • Data retention: How long are your prompts, responses, and uploaded files kept? What happens after the retention period?
  • Human review: Can vendor employees read your conversations? Under what circumstances?
  • Subprocessors: Who else gets your data? Third-party AI providers, cloud hosts, analytics vendors?
  • Advertising: Is your content used for ad targeting, even indirectly?
  • "Content" definition: What counts as "your content"? Does it include metadata, usage patterns, feedback?
  • Unilateral changes: Can the vendor change terms without notice? What's the notification period?
  • Legal process: Will the vendor notify you before responding to a subpoena or government request?
