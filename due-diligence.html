Review Methodology

Missouri Informal Opinion 2024-11 requires attorneys to "assess the terms and conditions of using a generative AI platform or service." ABA Formal Opinion 512 imposes affirmative duties to understand how AI tools process confidential information. This page documents that assessment.

For each provider document, we check 7 factors:

  1. Training — Does the provider train on your data? Can you opt out? Does the opt-out actually work?
  2. Retention — How long are prompts and responses retained? Is zero-data retention (ZDR) available?
  3. Human Review — Can employees or contractors read your conversations?
  4. Subprocessors — What third parties process your data? Is the provider truly bare metal?
  5. Legal Process — Will the vendor notify you before responding to a subpoena? What is their challenge posture?
  6. Advertising — Does chat data feed advertising or ad targeting?
  7. DPA Availability — Is a Data Processing Agreement available? At which tier?

Documents marked Prescreened have been reviewed by AI with key quotes and CTRL+F pointers extracted. A human reviewer must verify the quotes and update status to Reviewed. Documents marked Flagged have concerning or ambiguous language requiring closer analysis.

Bare-Metal Providers

These are the companies that actually build and run the AI models. Going direct to the provider minimizes the number of third parties handling your data.
