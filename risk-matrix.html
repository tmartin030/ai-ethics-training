Why This Page Exists

Missouri Opinion 2024-11 requires you to assess every AI tool's terms of service, data handling practices, and data sources before using it on client work. ABA Formal Opinion 512 goes further, imposing affirmative duties to understand how AI tools process confidential information. This page helps you do that assessment.

There is no risk-free tier. Every AI tool requires vigilance when working on client matters. The tiers below describe degrees of data protection, not levels of safety.

The 4 Tiers of Data Protection

Tier 1 — Consumer Default

Trains on your data. Lowest data protection.
ChatGPT Free/Plus • Claude Free/Pro/Max • Gemini personal • Perplexity Free/Pro • Copilot Free • Meta AI (no opt-out for ad targeting) • NotebookLM personal

Tier 2 — Consumer (Training Off)

Toggle training off. Still governed by consumer ToS.
ChatGPT with training OFF • Claude with training OFF • Gemini with Keep Activity OFF (weak opt-out: 72-hr retention, human review possible) • Perplexity with retention OFF

Tier 3 — API / DPA

Bilateral contract. No training on your data.
Anthropic API • OpenAI API • Perplexity Sonar API (ZDR) • Google Gemini API (paid) • Clio AI ($39/user/mo) • Reduct • Word Transcription • Paxton AI ($159–199/mo)

Tier 4 — Enterprise Tenancy

Data stays in a vendor-hosted tenant logically isolated to your organization.
M365 Copilot ($30/user/mo) • CoCounsel/Westlaw • Lexis+ AI • vLex/Vincent AI ($399/mo) • Harvey AI (~$1,000+/mo) • Google Workspace Gemini ($7/mo+) • NotebookLM Enterprise • Perplexity Enterprise Pro ($40/user/mo) • JusticeText
No tier is risk-free. Even at Tier 4, vendors retain carve-outs for legal process, safety/abuse detection, and TOS violations. "No human review" means no systematic review for training — it does not mean no human can ever access your data.

Memory Cross-Contamination

ChatGPT Memory is on by default. If you discuss Client A in one conversation, those facts can surface in a later conversation about Client B. This creates a dual ethical problem:

  • Rule 1.6 (Confidentiality) — Client A's information is disclosed without consent.
  • Rule 1.7 (Conflicts) — Information from one client influences advice to another.

Action: Turn memory off for all client work. In ChatGPT: Settings → Personalization → Memory → Off.

The Feedback Trap

When you click thumbs up or thumbs down on a response, you consent to that conversation being retained for training — for up to 5 years — even if you opted out of general training. OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google all do this.

Rule: Never provide feedback (thumbs up/down, ratings) on any conversation containing client information.

Consumer AI Chats Are Not Privileged

United States v. Heppner (S.D.N.Y. Feb. 2026). A client used consumer Claude AI for defense research. The court ordered production, finding "simply no reasonable expectation of confidentiality" under consumer terms of service. Enterprise and API tiers were distinguished.

Contractual Gotchas — What to Watch For

1. The Copilot Trap

Free Copilot looks identical to M365 Copilot but has completely different data protections. Same name, different legal framework. If your organization pays for M365 Copilot, confirm you are signed in to the enterprise version.

2. The Feedback Trap

Thumbs up/down on a response = consent to retain that conversation for training. This applies even when general training is toggled off. See The Feedback Trap above.

3. Gemini's Weak Opt-Out

72-hour retention after "opt-out," human review still possible during that window, and Google has silently re-enabled activity tracking after updates.

4. Meta AI — No Opt-Out

No opt-out at all for ad targeting based on your conversations. Meta AI should not be used for any work-related purpose.

5. Unilateral TOS Changes

Consumer tools can change their terms at any time with notice-only (or no notice). A tool that does not train today may train tomorrow. Monitor vendor announcements.

What to Ask Any AI Vendor

  1. Training: Do you train on my data? Can I opt out? Does the opt-out actually work?
  2. DPA: Do you offer a Data Processing Agreement?
  3. Retention: How long do you retain prompts and responses? Is zero-data retention (ZDR) available?
  4. Feedback: Does providing feedback cause conversations to be retained even if I opted out of training?
  5. Human review: Can your employees read my conversations?
  6. Subprocessors: What third-party models or services process my data?
  7. Certifications: SOC 2? ISO 27001? HIPAA BAA available?
  8. Legal process: Will you notify me before responding to a subpoena for my data?

Legal Process & Notification

Will your vendor tell you before handing over your data? Key takeaways:

Vendor Posture
Microsoft Most aggressive challenge posture. Redirected 62% of enterprise requests in latest transparency report.
Anthropic Strongest for API/enterprise. Redirects law enforcement to customer first.
OpenAI Notifies "to the extent legally permitted."
Google Notifies by email where permitted. Workspace has Access Transparency logging.
Perplexity Enterprise DPA only. No consumer notification. No transparency report.
Clio Prompt written notice, minimum-necessary standard.
Bottom line: No vendor can guarantee notification under gag orders. The best protection is zero-data retention (ZDR) — they cannot produce what they do not have.
