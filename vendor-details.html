Platform Vendors

These are the companies whose AI models power most legal AI tools. Understanding their data protections sets the baseline for evaluating every other vendor.

Microsoft
Tier 1-2 Tier 4
▶

The split

Microsoft runs two completely different AI ecosystems under the same brand. Copilot Free/Pro is a consumer product. M365 Copilot is an enterprise product. Same name, completely different data handling.

Products & pricing

Copilot Free
Tier 1 Free. Consumer terms. Data may be used for training. Uses conversation data for advertising — opting out of training does NOT opt out of ad use.
M365 E3 + Copilot
Tier 4 $42 + $30/user/mo (July 2026 pricing). Enterprise terms, DPA. No training. No ads. Data stays in your tenant.
M365 E5 + Copilot
Tier 4 $65 + $30/user/mo (July 2026 pricing). Everything in E3 plus Security Copilot features.
M365 Business Premium
Tier 4 ~$22 + $30/user/mo Small/mid-size option. Same enterprise data protections.
Azure OpenAI Service
Tier 3 Pay-per-token. Microsoft does not train on customer data. You can opt out of abuse monitoring. DPA available.

Data protection details

  • Training: M365 Copilot does NOT train on customer data. Prompts, responses, and Microsoft Graph data are never used to train foundation models.
  • DPA: Automatically included under Microsoft Products and Services Data Protection Addendum. Microsoft acts as data processor.
  • Data residency: EU Data Boundary available. Data stays in your M365 tenant.
  • Certifications: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 27018, FedRAMP High, HIPAA BAA, CJIS
  • Admin controls: Tenant admins control which Copilot features are enabled, who can use them, and what data sources Copilot can access.

How M365 meets ABA 512 requirements

Confidentiality (Rule 1.6)
Data stays in-tenant. No training. DPA with processor commitments. Encryption at rest (BitLocker) and in transit (TLS 1.2+). Tenant isolation.
Competence (Rule 1.1)
Copilot provides citations to source documents within your tenant. Attorney must still verify AI outputs.
Supervision (Rules 5.1-5.3)
Admin controls allow firms to restrict features by role. Usage logs available for audit. DLP policies apply to Copilot outputs.
Bottom line: M365 Copilot (with E3/E5 or Business Premium) is the gold standard for attorney data protection. If your office already pays for Microsoft 365, the $30/user/month Copilot add-on gets you Tier 4 AI across Word, Outlook, Teams, Excel, and PowerPoint with zero additional compliance work.
The trap: Copilot Free looks identical to M365 Copilot but has completely different data protections. It uses your conversations for advertising. If an attorney sees "Copilot" in their browser and starts using it for case work, they may be using the consumer version — not the enterprise one. Verify which version you're using.

Google
Tier 1 Tier 4

The split

Google also runs two separate AI ecosystems. Personal Gemini (gemini.google.com on a personal account) is consumer-grade. Google Workspace Gemini (even Business Starter at $7/mo) gets enterprise data protections. Same model, completely different data handling.

Products & pricing

Personal Gemini
Tier 1 Free. Trains on your data by default. "Keep Activity OFF" is a weak opt-out — 72-hour retention, possible human review, history of silently re-enabling.
Workspace Business Starter
Tier 4 $8.40/user/mo ($7/mo annual). Enterprise data protections. Gemini in Gmail, Docs, Sheets. No training. DPA included. 30 GB storage.
Workspace Business Standard
Tier 4 $16.80/user/mo ($14/mo annual). Full Gemini AI suite across all apps. 2 TB storage.
Workspace Business Plus
Tier 4 $26.40/user/mo ($22/mo annual). Advanced security + compliance. 5 TB storage.
Workspace Enterprise
Tier 4 Custom Unlimited storage. eDiscovery, Vault, advanced DLP. Custom terms.
NotebookLM (personal)
Tier 2 Free. Does not train on uploaded content. Some human review possible. Good for public documents only.
NotebookLM Enterprise
Tier 4 Full enterprise security. No human review.
Vertex AI (Google Cloud)
Tier 3 Pay-per-token. No training on customer data. DPA available. ISO 42001, FedRAMP High, HIPAA BAA.

Data protection details

  • Training: Workspace Gemini does NOT train on customer data. Content is not human reviewed. This applies to all Workspace plans, even Business Starter.
  • DPA: Cloud Data Processing Addendum automatically included for all Workspace plans. Google acts as data processor.
  • Data residency: Available on Business Plus and Enterprise. Choose specific regions for data at rest.
  • Certifications: SOC 2 Type II, ISO 27001, ISO 42001 (AI-specific), FedRAMP High, HIPAA BAA, BSI C5
  • Admin controls: Workspace admins can enable/disable Gemini features per organizational unit. Detailed audit logs.
  • DPIA support: Google has published a formal DPIA resource for organizations assessing Gemini in Workspace.

How Workspace meets ABA 512 requirements

Confidentiality (Rule 1.6)
Data stays in Workspace tenant. No training. DPA with processor commitments. Not used for advertising. Encryption at rest and in transit.
Competence (Rule 1.1)
Gemini in Docs/Gmail provides AI assistance within your existing documents. Attorney must verify all AI suggestions.
Supervision (Rules 5.1-5.3)
Admin console controls per org unit. Usage reporting and audit logs. DLP rules apply to Gemini-generated content.
Bottom line: Google Workspace Business Starter at $7/mo/user is the most affordable path to Tier 4 AI. If your office uses Gmail and Google Docs, you already have the infrastructure — you just need the Workspace plan instead of a personal Google account.
The trap: Personal Gemini exists within the broader Google ad ecosystem. Even if chat content is walled off from ads, Google knows your search history, YouTube activity, location, and browsing habits. Workspace completely separates your data from Google's consumer ad profile. The difference is the plan, not the URL.

Tier 3 $5/$25 per M tokens Same protections. Most capable model.

Tier 3 $1/$5 per M tokens Fastest/cheapest. Same protections.

Data protection details

  • Training (consumer): Training on by default since Sept 28, 2025. Toggle off in Settings → Data Privacy Controls. Opted-out data retained 30 days. Opted-in data retained up to 5 years (de-identified).
  • Training (commercial/API): NO training on customer data. Exception: voluntary Developer Partner Program opt-in.
  • Feedback trap: Thumbs up/down feedback retained for 5 years even if you've opted out of general training. Feedback = consent to train on that conversation.
  • DPA: Automatically included with commercial terms (Team, Enterprise, API). No separate signing needed. Includes EU SCCs.
  • ZDR: Available for Enterprise accounts. Must request through sales. Immediate deletion after abuse checks.
  • API log retention: 7 days (reduced from 30 days in Sept 2025).
  • No advertising: No ads, no ad-supported tiers, no ad-targeting of any kind.

Claude Code & Claude Cowork

Your login method determines your tier. Claude Code and Cowork authenticated with a consumer Pro/Max subscription follow Consumer Terms (Tier 1/2). Authenticated with an API key, they follow Commercial Terms (Tier 3). The product is the same — the legal framework changes based on how you log in. See Agents page for full details.

For MSPD: If you use Claude Pro/Max for case-related work, at minimum toggle training off (Settings → Data Privacy). Better: use an API key ($5-25/M tokens for the best model). Best: request Team or Enterprise plan with DPA.

What to Ask Any AI Vendor

Missouri Opinion 2024-11 requires you to assess the terms of service, security of inputs, and data sources. Use this checklist when evaluating any AI tool for legal work:

1. Training
Do you train on my data? Can I opt out? Does opting out actually work (or do you retain data anyway for "product improvement")?
2. DPA
Do you offer a Data Processing Agreement? Is it automatically included or do I need to request it?
3. Retention
How long do you retain my prompts and responses? What is the retention period for logs? Is ZDR available?
4. Feedback
If I provide feedback (thumbs up/down), does that conversation get retained for training even if I've opted out?
5. Human review
Can your employees read my conversations? Under what circumstances? Safety review only, or general product improvement?
6. Subprocessors
What third-party AI models do you use? What are their data protections? (If a legal AI tool uses OpenAI's API, what are OpenAI's terms?)
7. Certifications
SOC 2? ISO 27001? HIPAA? FedRAMP? What independent audits have you completed?
8. Legal process
Will you notify me before responding to a subpoena or government data request? (Most vendors have carve-outs for law enforcement requests without notification.)
